# SOS ballot status reports (archive)

Copies of the California Secretary of State's ballot status reports (`bsr-statistics-*.xlsx`).
SOS takes these down after each election, so the SOS fetch (`fetch_ballot_returns.py`) saves a
copy of every report it downloads here.

Each report has, per county: VBM issued; VBM returned by method (drop box, drop-off location,
vote center drop-off, mail, fax, other); VBM accepted; in-person ballots cast (regular,
provisional, conditional CVR, "instant" CVR) and accepted; and total ballots cast.

- `2025-special/bsr-statistics-11-03-25-final.*` — final pre-election report (Nov 3 2025, 9:09 pm), E−1.
- `2026-general/` — 2026 General reports from Oct 31 onward.

Reports for the 2022 General, 2024 Primary/General and 2026 Primary could not be found on the
SOS site or in the Internet Archive.
